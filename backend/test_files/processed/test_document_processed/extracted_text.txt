AI-Assisted Academic Document Verification
Sample Research Paper for Preprocessing Tests
Abstract
Academic document verification involves examining digital documents for textual consistency, structural
information, and signs of possible manipulation. Automated preprocessing prepares documents for later
OCR, information extraction, similarity analysis, and authenticity verification.
1. Introduction
Educational institutions increasingly use digital documents such as research papers, academic reports,
certificates, and marksheets. A reliable verification system should accept different file formats and prepare
their content for subsequent processing stages.
2. Methodology
The proposed pipeline first identifies the uploaded file type. Text-based documents can be processed directly,
while scanned pages can be converted into images and prepared for OCR. Image preprocessing may include
resizing, grayscale conversion, noise reduction, contrast enhancement, and deskewing.
